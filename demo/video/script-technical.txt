LIQUID: ONE-MINUTE TECHNICAL WALKTHROUGH (about 155 words, spoken)

Under the hood, Liquid is one orchestrator with five external apps as tools.

The seller texts a photo. BlueBubbles delivers it over a webhook and a poller, and every
message is fingerprinted, so delayed redeliveries never trigger a second reply. Today that
caught thirty-two duplicates and refused ten messages from a number not on the allowlist.

OpenAI vision identifies the item. An image model produces the listing photo, then a second
call compares it against the original and blocks any edit that changes the product or hides a
defect. The seller still has to approve it.

Apify pulls sold and active eBay comps. Those feed a deterministic pricing engine, no LLM in
the loop, that solves for the seller's deadline and floor, and reprices on its own clock.

On "go", the eBay Sell API creates the inventory item and offer. Gmail watches for offers and
sales. Every action writes a ledger row.

Twelve invariants, one hundred seventy-two tests, twelve thousand simulation runs, zero
violations.
